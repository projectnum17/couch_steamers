How Often Should You Clean for Allergy Relief?

There’s no one rule for everyone, but here’s a simple starting point:

  • Couch and chairs: every 6–12 months
  • Rugs and carpets: every 6–12 months, depending on traffic
  • Mattress: every 6–18 months, depending on allergies and pets

If you have:

  • kids and pets,
  • year-round allergy symptoms,
  • or a history of asthma and sensitivity,

Lean toward the shorter end of those ranges.
